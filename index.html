The House

Every jewel arrives with a history

Heritage Antique Jewels exists for people who believe a jewel should have a story before it has an owner. We source from estates, auctions and private collections across Europe — then research, authenticate, and sensitively restore each piece before it enters the vitrine.

I

Sourced

Found in estates and private collections, chosen for beauty, condition and character.

II

Authenticated

Inspected, tested and documented. Condition report and provenance notes with every piece.

III

Delivered

Sensitively restored where needed, then shipped worldwide — fully insured and tracked.
